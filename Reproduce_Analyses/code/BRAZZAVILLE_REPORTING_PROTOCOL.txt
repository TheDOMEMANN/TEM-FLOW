BRAZZAVILLE RETROSPECTIVE REPORTING COMPARISON
Protocol fixed on 5 October 2026 before computing comparison outcomes.

Question
Do two equal-size reporting arrangements preserve different amounts of useful
market-level information when one archived report is lost? This evaluates
reporting-packet resilience using African survey quantities. It is not a field
trial, an independent validation of measurement accuracy, or a test of newly
acquired backup measurements.

Source and eligibility
Ameller, J., Moustier, P., Maba Ngouloubi, P.L., and Ofoueme-Berton, Y. (2026).
Dataset of produce supply flows in Brazzaville: A survey of traders in 2022.
CIRAD Dataverse, version 1, https://doi.org/10.18167/DVN1/DAMXAW.
The retail CSV contains 8,208 rows, 14 market labels and 16 vegetables in Rainy
and Dry seasons. Use all finite, nonnegative published volume_kg values.
Require each product's positive total in both seasons. List all exclusions.
Do not infer unsampled city flows. A market-product cell without an eligible
row has zero recorded quantity in this finite sample, not proven physical zero.
Market labels are used exactly as in the CSV. The README's example market list
differs from the CSV; no unsupported name reconciliation will be introduced.

Temporal separation
According to the source README, the rainy and dry collections were in April
and August 2022. Choose arrangements using only Rainy quantities; evaluate on
Dry quantities. These are two survey snapshots, not a time series or random
sample of future reporting conditions. The same respondents may recur because
participant identities are unavailable. This is an internally fixed analysis
protocol, not an externally preregistered or independently blinded study.

Two arrangements, fixed scalar-report budget
Both arrangements retain one exact archived total of the Dry survey sample and
seven scalar report packets. Neither receives more scalar reports.
A: individual-market reporting. Report the seven markets with largest Rainy
quantity for that product. Break equal-quantity ties by the CSV market name.
B: paired-market reporting. Rank markets by Rainy quantity; pair largest with
smallest, second-largest with second-smallest, and so on. Report seven disjoint
pair totals. These pairs are accounting groups, not physical connections.
The scalar-report budget is not a claim about equal collection effort or cost.

Loss and dependence
Evaluate no loss and each of the seven possible single-packet losses exactly.
The common archived total is always available. Loss occurs after reports were
aggregated, during hypothetical storage or transmission. It is NOT deletion of
an original survey row while pretending its derived aggregates are independent.
All reports and the archived total derive from the same survey quantities.
They share unit-price and unit-weight conversion errors. Do not model them as
independent acquisitions or invent independent noise. Exact constraints refer
only to published numerical quantities, not to physical measurement accuracy.

Calculations and validation
Constrain nonnegative market allocations to the archived sample total and to
every surviving report. Calculate each market's minimum and maximum allocation.
Check every bound against independent scipy.optimize.linprog solutions.
Use the unchanged production_tree module to calculate prospective widths for
zero or one lost report. Compare every conditional width with the corresponding
prospective width; preserve equal prospective results and negative findings.
Refer to the Dry aggregate as the source-data reference, not ground truth.
Containment is a consistency check guaranteed by construction; it is not
empirical coverage evidence.

Outcomes (all are reported)
Primary: mean width across the 14 markets, divided by the sample total, under
the worst single-loss state for each product and arrangement. Summarize the
16 paired product outcomes with median, range, and win/tie/loss counts.
Secondary: mean width across all seven one-loss states (equal weights describe
the scenario average and are not an estimated loss probability).
For thresholds 5%, 10%, 25%, and 50% of the sample total, report all thresholds
separately. At or above means lower bound >= threshold; below means upper
bound < threshold. Use numerical tolerance 1e-10 on normalized shares.
Report no-loss resolution, equal-weight
one-loss scenario resolution, and robust resolution (a market has the same
resolved conclusion in every no-loss and single-loss state). Separate above
from below so absence conclusions cannot be mistaken for positive detection.
No thresholds are policy or health thresholds. No outcome-guided threshold,
product, market, loss-state or design selection is allowed.

Stopping and incorporation
Stop after all eligible products, two arrangements, eight states, fourteen
markets and four thresholds have been evaluated and checks completed. Preserve
all product-level and scenario-level outputs, including any worse arrangement.
Incorporate only as retrospective African survey-data evaluation. The remaining
gap is independent validation of reporting acquisition, actual report-loss
mechanisms, measurement-error allowances and operational collection costs.

Prescoring amendment
Before any outcome calculation, threshold equality was harmonized with the
existing manuscript's 'at least' convention (>=), replacing strict >.
The initial protocol and its hash are preserved in the workflow record.
The seven-pair design's mean width is algebraically 2/14 of the sample total:
each pair sum is the upper bound of its two members, including after any one
pair report is lost and recovered from the archived total. This identity is
not an empirical discovery. Product comparisons and threshold conclusions
still depend on the recorded quantities. No other design rule was changed.

# TEM-FLOW 1.0.0 algorithm lineage

TEM-FLOW 1.0.0 is the first manuscript-aligned software release of the current
TEM admissible-claim calculus. The mathematical operator is inherited without
alteration from the validated TEM-FLOW 0.3 canonical implementation.

The authoritative formulation and proof documents therefore retain their
frozen names:

- `TEMFLOW_MATHEMATICAL_FORMULATION_V0_3.md`
- `TEMFLOW_FORMAL_PROOFS_V0_3.md`

Those filenames identify the mathematical revision that was proved and
validated. They do not mean that the installed Python distribution is version
0.3.0. The installed distribution, API, command-line program, and interface are
TEM-FLOW 1.0.0.

## Constitutive algorithm retained in 1.0.0

1. Quantitative evidence is represented as identity-indexed non-negative
   intervals with immutable dependency identifiers and epistemic status.
2. Addition is defined only within identical scientific signatures.
3. Every identity-changing yield, conversion, allocation, denominator, route,
   or path operation is a partial map backed by an explicit certificate.
4. Two-margin route cells and three-margin source-checkpoint-destination cells
   use sharp closed-form admissible intervals.
5. The least admissible-claim closure retains complete dependency witnesses.
6. Missing or incompatible obligations produce typed blockers rather than
   inferred zeros or silently coerced point estimates.
7. Current-state and historical analyses select compatible temporal evidence
   independently by stream; same-date evidence is not required.

## Version-promotion changes

The 1.0.0 promotion changes product identity, packaging metadata, documentation,
and manuscript framing. It makes multi-domain mass-flow reconstruction the
primary scope and treats CPC, EDI, HQ, and related indicators as optional
certificate-gated consumers of valid upstream claims. It does not change the
constitutive mathematics or relabel frozen evidence.

## Provenance boundary

Ledger rows and certificates retain
`TEMFLOW_Patterns_private_engine_v0_3_0_2026-09-01` as their source release.
Their hashes and proof digests are part of the validation chain and must not be
rewritten merely because the software wrapper is promoted to 1.0.0. The
original ED-FLOW 1.0.0 software and ledger also remain unchanged.
